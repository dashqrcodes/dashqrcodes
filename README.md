### Keeping People From Being Forgotten

People die and become forgotten. 200 years ago was the first photo. People use photos to remember our loved ones: we make montages and slideshows and post them on social media. People remember life with photos. We look at a photo and instantly our minds take us back to that moment. In that instant...that photo becomes a time machine. We can remember emotions, tastes, sounds, smells, and feelings. A photo can do so much for us, but what a photo can't do is allow others to relive what that instant meant to each one of us. 

On the 200th anniversary of the first photo, we are embarking on building something people didn't know they wanted. We are reinventing the photo.


### Origin Story

People die all the time, and sadly most become forgotten. I was exposed to this reality in my previouse career in firefighting and EMS in LA. 

As a first responder I would see people die on a daily basis. We walked into people's homes and would see where they took their final breaths. I would see all of their photos on the walls; their entire life timeline. Baby pictures, wedding pictures, graduations, vacations, and I wondered, "after this person's funeral...what happens to all of these photos, these memories of their life?" The sad reality is those pictures end up in a box or in the cloud forever to become forgotten! That realization was painful and lives with me to this day. That pain is what drives this mission. After 23 years of firefighting and emergency response in Los Angeles I retired, taking a smaller pension, to launch this start up to keep people from being forgotten. 

### Why The Startup. 

June 3, 2017 my friend and fellow firefighter Kelly Wong, 29, died in the line of duty. To keep him from being forgotten, I had the idea to put his photo tribute slideshow on an engraved QR code on his memorial at the firehouse. That was more complicated than it needed to be. DIY solutions, even with AI, have friction. DASH is removing that friction and making a beautiful way to be remembered.

### No Funding. Bootstrapped. Validation MVP Zero to One

I partnered with a local L.A. mortuary determine whether or not poeple would pay for this. I used analog methods (wix website https://dashqrcodes.com , Canva, Google photos, YouTube, qr app, video editing apps all on mobile!!!) Lots of pain. 

Results:

- 400+ paying customers
- 30,000+ printed cards and keepsakes
- $70K lifetime revenue
- 98% customer satisfaction (2% were hard lessons we apply to QC today)
- 30+ old photos animated with AI — families saw their loved ones young and free... in "heaven"

I built a mobile web app to automate everything I'v been doing analog. This is not the AI end product in our product roadmap, but it is the next step that makes money. It is a print-on-demand mobile application that let's customers instantly create, order, and add their loved ones slideshows onto their memorial service cards and canvas portrait keepsakes all within one application. I made it to save my sanity, the funeral home's sanity, the print shop's sanity, and most importantly...the end customer honoring their loved one. 

MVP https://dashmemories.com 

### A Boring Business Opportunity: 

Global printing industry is $500B. Global printing specifically in life memories is $56B. 

### EASTER EGG: the same building where Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak sold their first 200 Apple Computers is the same building where I fulfilled my first order of QR printed postcards. 

The funeral home customers can use their phones to easily create a physical memorial keepsake, such as memorial cards, a canvas portrait, and instantly add photos and videos to those physical keepsakes all in one app. Others will then be able to scan the keepsakes to share memories of a life. 

This is merely the beginning. The world's biggest fires all started with a spark.
